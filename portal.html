02 Brand kit

How WEA looks and sounds.

Use these assets consistently. When in doubt: deep green, heritage gold, a display headline and a sans body. Never recolour or stretch the mark.

Primary mark

The WEA monogram. Minimum width 96 px. Keep clear space equal to one radius of the mark on every side.

WEA-2026-000001 IBM Plex Mono — IDs, codes & labels

Voice

Do

  • Speak with quiet authority — the network speaks for itself.
  • Name the member and spotlight their venture.
  • Use active verbs: build, launch, connect, verify.
  • Cite the global footprint — countries, sectors, outcomes.

Don't

  • Overpromise or use hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
  • Recolour, rotate or stretch the monogram.
  • Post without a clear call to action.
  • Share member data beyond what the public directory shows.
