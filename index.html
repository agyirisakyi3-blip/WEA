01 Our mission

Five commitments.
One mission.

To connect, equip, empower and promote entrepreneurs worldwide — turning local businesses into global enterprises.

We connect members to the right global partners, investors and markets — because no entrepreneur should have to build alone. That means introductions made by the Secretariat, matched to your sector and stage, and a directory that lets the rest of the network find you.

We equip members with modern entrepreneurial skills, leadership training and digital technology. Clinics and workshops run across the chapters and online, so what you learn in Accra on Monday is still useful in Nairobi on Friday.

We empower members with access to funding, grants, loans and investment capital. Members are guided through what lenders and investors actually ask for — financials, valuation, documentation — long before they sit across the table.

We promote members' businesses and brands on global platforms, conferences and media. A verified listing, a profile in the directory and a page in WEA channels — the difference between being unknown and being findable.

We transform local businesses into global enterprises — creating wealth and jobs at national scale. A trader in Kumasi and a manufacturer in Lagos are not separate stories: they are the same story, told in different currencies.

04 — The Charter of Values

What holds us together.

Seven articles form the pillars upon which the association is built — in every chapter, every deal, every decision.

These are not aspirations posted on a wall. They are the terms on which members deal with one another, and the reason a WEA introduction carries weight: the person you are introduced to has agreed to the same seven articles you have.

Articles I – VII

  1. I.

    Integrity

    Honesty, transparency and the fear of God in all we do.

  2. II.

    Excellence

    Distinction in every product, service and programme.

  3. III.

    Unity

    One global family of entrepreneurs — unity in diversity.

  4. IV.

    Innovation

    Creativity, technology and bold new solutions.

  5. V.

    Collaboration

    We grow through partnership, not competition.

  6. VI.

    Service

    Serving humanity and transforming communities.

  7. VII.

    Legacy

    Businesses that outlive us and bless generations.

05 — The Roadmap · 2026 – 2036

A ten-year blueprint,
phase by phase.

Ten million entrepreneurs in one hundred countries is not a slogan; it is a schedule. Each phase sets a target, and each phase is reported against it — so members can see where the association actually stands, not just where it intends to be.

2026

Phase I

Foundation & Structuring

Target · 100K members · 20 countries
  • Global registration and legal structuring
  • Global Secretariat established in Accra, Ghana
  • Inauguration of Board of Trustees and Executive Council
  • Membership drive launch across 20 countries
  • WEA website, app and membership portal
2028

Phase II

Expansion & Visibility

Target · 1 million members
  • Fifty country chapters established
  • Annual Global Entrepreneurs Conference & Awards
  • WEA Business Funding and Grant Program
  • WEA Magazine and Business Directory published
2030

Phase III

Empowerment & Trade

Target · 3M members · 75 countries
  • WEA Trade Fair and Exhibition Centre
  • Investment bank / microfinance for members
  • E-commerce platform for inter-member trade
  • Online Entrepreneurship University
2032

Phase IV

Industrialization & Impact

Target · Jobs at national scale
  • Industrial park and manufacturing hubs
  • WEA Humanitarian Foundation launched
  • 10,000 jobs created per country chapter
  • Partnerships with UN, AU, World Bank and Fortune 500
2036

Phase V

Legacy & Global Leadership

Target · 10M verified members · 100 countries
  • Global authority in entrepreneurship policy
  • Presence in 100 countries with 10 million members
  • World Entrepreneurs House headquarters
  • Legacy Fund for next-generation entrepreneurs
