03 How verification works

Built for third parties.

01

Every member is registered

Applications are reviewed by the Secretariat. Approved members receive a unique member ID and a private verification code.

02

Anyone can check a record

Members share their ID or a verification link. The lookup returns name, venture, class of membership and live standing.

03

Status stays current

Verified or pending — the badge always reflects the member's standing with the association at the moment of the check.
